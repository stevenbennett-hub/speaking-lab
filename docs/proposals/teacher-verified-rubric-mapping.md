This page maps your provisional framework (Slice 10) onto the rubrics already published on the course site. Those rubrics are the `RUBRICS` table in `site/index.html`, which shows the same rubrics students see under **Rubrics**.

**No criteria or scales were invented.**
- Your rubrics use five levels: **Exceeds · Meets · Approaching · Beginning · Missing**.
- They have no level called "proficient". Where the framework says *proficient*, this page reads it as **Meets or Exceeds**. That is a proposal for you to confirm or change.

> Check first: confirm that the Canvas rubrics you grade with match these site rubrics (names, points and levels). If they differ, the Canvas rubric wins, and this mapping should be redone from it.

## Academic Integrity gate (all speeches)
Every speech rubric has the required, unscored **Academic Integrity** gate (W.8). Recommendation: a speech counts toward any badge only when the gate is **met**. The rubric's own wording for "met":
> "Disclosure complete; any AI use only refined the student's own work; sources put in own words, with quotes and credit."

## 1. Confident Speaker
*Framework:* proficient delivery on at least two formal speech presentations.

**Formal speeches** (proposed): Heritage Month (informative, Unit 3), How-To/Demonstration (Unit 4), Narrative (Unit 5), Persuasive (Unit 6).
- The Self-Introduction (Unit 1, 1–2 min) is a first speech. It is left out unless you want it included.
- The Research & Outline Lab (Unit 2) has no delivery rows.

**Delivery rows** that already exist (high school rubrics):

| Speech | Rows | Meets or Exceeds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Informative, Demonstration | Audience Connection & Preparation · Voice · Confidence & Engagement | 8 or 10 of 10, each row |
| Persuasive | Audience Connection & Preparation · Voice · Confidence & Persuasiveness | 8 or 10 of 10, each row |
| Narrative (PVLEGS) | Poise · Voice · Life · Eye Contact & Gestures | 8 or 10 of 10, each row |

**Recommended threshold:** Meets or Exceeds on **every** delivery row listed for that speech, on **two or more** of the four formal speeches.

**Left out on purpose**:
- **Time.** It measures length, not delivery.
- **Clarity in English.** It is a language criterion, and its own descriptor says accents are never scored. Counting it toward a "confident speaker" badge could disadvantage students learning English.

Add either one if you prefer.

## 2. Master Storyteller
*Framework:* proficient narrative organization, delivery and audience engagement on the narrative speech.

Narrative Speech rubric (Unit 5), existing rows:

| Framework part | Rubric row(s) | Meets (rubric wording) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Narrative organization | **Clear Structure** (10) | "All parts present; one is thin or a little out of order" |
| Delivery | **Poise · Voice · Life · Eye Contact & Gestures** (10 each) | e.g. Voice: "Audible; tone or pace could vary more" |
| Audience engagement | **Engaging Storytelling** (10) | "Good details; a few parts are told instead of shown" |

**Recommended threshold:** Meets or Exceeds (8 or 10) on all six rows above, Academic Integrity met.

Not used:
- Focus & Purpose, Personal Insight and Preparation & Effort (reflection and effort, not performance).
- Time.

## 3. Master Communicator
*Framework:* proficient communication performance across the informative, demonstration, narrative and persuasive speeches.

**Two options**, both using only existing rubric points:
- **A. Row-based (recommended):** on each of the four speeches, Meets or Exceeds on every delivery row listed for Confident Speaker, **and** on the speech's main content rows:
  - Informative: Introduction, Main Points & Information, Sources & Oral Citations, Conclusion.
  - Demonstration: Introduction, Steps, Props & Demonstration, Audience Can Follow, Conclusion.
  - Narrative: Clear Structure, Engaging Storytelling, Focus & Purpose.
  - Persuasive: Introduction, Arguments & Evidence, Counterargument & Rebuttal, Conclusion.
- **B. Total-based:** on each of the four speeches, a total at least equal to the sum of the rubric's "Meets" points:

| Speech (HS rubric) | Total points | Sum of "Meets" points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Heritage Month (informative) | 100 | 82 |
| How-To / Demonstration | 100 | 82 |
| Narrative | 100 | 80 |
| Persuasive | 100 | 83 |

Option B is easier to read from Canvas totals, but a strong content score can hide weak delivery. Option A matches the badge's meaning better. Either way, Academic Integrity must be met on all four.
