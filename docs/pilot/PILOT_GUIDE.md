# Speaking Lab 2.0 — Classroom pilot guide (one high school Speech class)

**For:** Mr. Bennett.
**Plan:** one 90-minute block, about 45 minutes of it on Speaking Lab.
**Where:** the preview site, or production once approved.

**Goal:** see whether students can find today's lesson, do the routine, use one activity and keep their progress. It is not a test of the students.

## Before class (15 min)
- [ ] Open the site on a district laptop on the school network: `https://stevenbennett-hub.github.io/speaking-lab-preview/` (or the production address after approval).
  - The preview shows a yellow **DEVELOPMENT PREVIEW** bar. Its progress is separate from the real site.
- [ ] If the pilot falls in Unit 1, open **Lesson Manager → Playbook** and choose the pilot date. You get a timed 90-minute plan, materials, prep and the formative resources.
- [ ] Pick the day you will pilot. In the preview, any date can be opened with `?date=YYYY-MM-DD`.
- [ ] Decide which interactive activity everyone will try. Suggestions:
  - Unit 1: Beyond the Podium, **Read the Room** and **Watch the Film**.
  - Unit 6: **Ad Detective, Case 1**. Its preview needs `ad-detective-preview`; otherwise use production Ad Detective after approval.
  - Any unit: one **Practice Lab** activity.
- [ ] Have paper journals ready. Know which students have the typed-journal permission; give the code only to them, privately.
- [ ] Print or project the anonymous feedback questionnaire (`STUDENT_FEEDBACK.md`).

## In class
| Min | Step | What to say / do | Watch for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5 | **Introduce Speaking Lab** | "This is our course website. Every class day has a lesson with today's goals, a journal and activities. Your work saves in this browser, not in an account. It is practice, not a grade; Canvas is where grades live." | Who can't load it (network, filter) |
| 5–10 | **Choose a learning pathway** | On the welcome screen students choose: *I am a speech student* (High School Speech) or *I am learning English* (same course, with language supports). They can change it anytime. College stays "Not offered at this time". | Confusion between pathway and level |
| 10–12 | **Language support** | In **Preferences** (top right), **Language support** has three settings: *Guided* (English and Spanish side by side, vocabulary open), *Supported*, and *Independent* (supports folded). "This is a preference, not a level. The goals are the same for everyone." | Students who switch back and forth (fine) |
| 12–15 | **Enter the unit** | Click **Today's Lesson** in the header: it opens today's lesson for the current unit. The first time, students pick their class periods (odd or even days). From then on, Today's Lesson is the daily starting point. All six units are on **My Course**. | Wrong period group, which gives wrong journal numbers |
| 15–18 | **Today's objectives** | Read the learning objective and the language objective aloud ("I can…"). Point to the success criteria. | Can students say the goal in their own words? |
| 18–28 | **Handwritten journal** | Journal #N and its prompt are on Today. "10 minutes, by hand, in your journal." Students with permission may type instead: the code unlocks it on that browser only. | Number matches your journal sets |
| 28–33 | **Stand and share** | "Stand up and share with someone new," using the prompt in the **Stand and share** box. | Partner instructions understood |
| 33–43 | **Interactive activity** | Everyone opens the chosen activity from **Labs and studios** and finishes one part. | Where students get stuck; loading time of the film |
| 43–46 | **Achievements** | Header → **My Achievements**. Badges show what is needed and what is done. "Speaking badges are verified by me in Canvas, not online. Learning points count each skill once." | Do they understand "practice, not grades"? |
| 46–50 | **Back up progress** | **Preferences** (top right) → **Back up or restore my progress** → **Download my backup file** → save to Google Drive. "Shared laptops can be cleared." | Download blocked? Drive save works? |
| 50–55 | **Feedback** | Anonymous questionnaire (paper or a Canvas survey with names off). | |

**Ad Detective keeps its own backup** (Notebook → Download a backup). The Speaking Lab backup doesn't include it.

## After class (10 min)
- Note what blocked students: loading, the filter, wrong group, the language toggle, Ad Detective link, download.
- Tally the questionnaire. Bring the top three problems to the next review. Fix those before a wider release.
- Record results in `docs/RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md`, section "Student classroom pilot".

## Privacy reminders
- No accounts, no names on the questionnaire, and no student data sent anywhere by the site.
- Don't ask students to say why they need typed journals or language supports.
- Don't collect screenshots that show student names or Canvas information.


## Slice 12 additions (the unified application)
- **Learning Labs** in the header opens the activity library, not the long course page. Students open a lab from its card or from Today's Lesson. **← Learning Labs** brings them back.
- **Resources** in the header opens the resource library: textbook, model speeches and videos, Voices of Change, rubrics, research and citation, the AI policy, pacing, due dates and course information.
- **Preferences** (top right) holds the pathway, class periods, language, language support, light/dark mode, sound and motion, and the progress backup.
- **Sound is off by default.** Leave it that way for the pilot. If a student turns it on, **Quiet Classroom** in Preferences, or **Mute sounds** in the header, turns everything off at once.
- **Appearance:** first-time visitors see dark mode; students who used the site before keep the light look. Either can be changed in Preferences → Appearance.
- **Teacher view code:** a classroom convenience that opens the Lesson Manager and future units on your browser. It is not a password or secure sign-in; anyone who learns it can use it. Change it if students learn it.
- **Celebrations.** When a student earns a badge during class, a short card appears. Students close it with Continue or Escape. It appears once per badge, never again after a refresh or a backup restore. To show the class what it looks like without awarding anything, use **Teacher tools → `#/demo/celebrate`**.
- **Watch for:**
  - Can students get from Today's Lesson to a lab and back without help?
  - Do they find Resources?
  - Does anyone end up on the long classic page by accident?

## Portable achievements (optional pilot step)
- On **My Achievements**, a student with an earned badge opens **Certificate** and tries **Print** (printer friendly) or **Download PDF**. The name is optional.
- Point out the privacy note next to the name: it is not saved and not sent.
- Show **Email to myself** once: the student types their own address and attaches the file. Speaking Lab never sends email.
- **Watch for:**
  - Does the school printer print the low-ink certificate on one page?
  - Does an email app open on the district MacBooks, or do students need the copy buttons with webmail?
