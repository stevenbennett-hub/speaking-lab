# Speaking Lab 2.0 — Classroom pilot guide (one high school Speech class)

**For:** Mr. Bennett.
**Plan:** one 90-minute block, about 45 minutes of it on Speaking Lab.
**Where:** the preview site, or production once approved.

**Goal:** see whether students can find today's lesson, do the routine, use one activity and keep their progress. It is not a test of the students.

## Before class (15 min)
- [ ] Open the site on a district laptop on the school network: `https://stevenbennett-hub.github.io/speaking-lab-preview/` (or the production address after approval).
  - The preview shows a yellow **DEVELOPMENT PREVIEW** bar. Its progress is separate from the real site.
- [ ] Pick the day you will pilot. In the preview, any date can be opened with `?date=YYYY-MM-DD`.
- [ ] Decide which interactive activity everyone will try. Suggestions:
  - Unit 1: Beyond the Podium, **Read the Room** and **Watch the Film**.
  - Unit 6: **Ad Detective, Case 1**. Its preview needs `ad-detective-preview`; otherwise use production Ad Detective after approval.
  - Any unit: one **Practice Lab** activity.
- [ ] Have paper journals ready. Know which students have the typed-journal permission; give the code only to them, privately.
- [ ] Print or project the anonymous feedback questionnaire (`STUDENT_FEEDBACK.md`).

## In class
| Min | Step | What to say / do | Watch for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5 | **Introduce Speaking Lab** | "This is our course website. Every class day has a lesson with today's goals, a journal and activities. Your work saves in this browser, not in an account. It is practice, not a grade; Canvas is where grades live." | Who can't load it (network, filter) |
| 5–10 | **Choose a learning pathway** | On the welcome screen students choose: *I am a speech student* (High School Speech) or *I am learning English* (same course, with language supports). They can change it anytime. College stays "Not offered at this time". | Confusion between pathway and level |
| 10–12 | **Language support** | On My course, **Language support** has three settings: *Guided* (English and Spanish side by side, vocabulary open), *Supported*, and *Independent* (supports folded). "This is a preference, not a level. The goals are the same for everyone." | Students who switch back and forth (fine) |
| 12–15 | **Enter the unit** | My course → the current unit card. The **Today** tab opens. Students also pick their class periods (odd or even days) once. | Wrong period group, which gives wrong journal numbers |
| 15–18 | **Today's objectives** | Read the learning objective and the language objective aloud ("I can…"). Point to the success criteria. | Can students say the goal in their own words? |
| 18–28 | **Handwritten journal** | Journal #N and its prompt are on Today. "10 minutes, by hand, in your journal." Students with permission may type instead: the code unlocks it on that browser only. | Number matches your journal sets |
| 28–33 | **Stand and share** | "Stand up and share with someone new," using the prompt in the **Stand and share** box. | Partner instructions understood |
| 33–43 | **Interactive activity** | Everyone opens the chosen activity from **Labs and studios** and finishes one part. | Where students get stuck; loading time of the film |
| 43–46 | **Achievements** | My course → **My achievements**. Badges show what is needed and what is done. "Speaking badges are verified by me in Canvas, not online. Learning points count each skill once." | Do they understand "practice, not grades"? |
| 46–50 | **Back up progress** | Top bar → **My work** → **Download my backup file** → save to Google Drive. "Shared laptops can be cleared." | Download blocked? Drive save works? |
| 50–55 | **Feedback** | Anonymous questionnaire (paper or a Canvas survey with names off). | |

**Ad Detective keeps its own backup** (Notebook → Download a backup). The Speaking Lab backup doesn't include it.

## After class (10 min)
- Note what blocked students: loading, the filter, wrong group, the language toggle, Ad Detective link, download.
- Tally the questionnaire. Bring the top three problems to the next review. Fix those before a wider release.
- Record results in `docs/RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md`, section "Student classroom pilot".

## Privacy reminders
- No accounts, no names on the questionnaire, and no student data sent anywhere by the site.
- Don't ask students to say why they need typed journals or language supports.
- Don't collect screenshots that show student names or Canvas information.
